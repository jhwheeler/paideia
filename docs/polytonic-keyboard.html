Colored keys are dead keys: press them before the vowel — nothing appears until the vowel lands. Small top-left glyph = Shift; small bottom-right = RAlt (third level). Only ] has a standalone form (] + Space → ͺ); for the elision mark use the apostrophe ' at RAlt⇧0.
